Quick answer

The cheapest proven stack is DIY generic finasteride plus minoxidil, mainstream telehealth like Keeps and Hims sits in the middle, and compounded premium topicals top the table. Every price on this page was verified in July 2026.

Hair loss treatment is a multi-year subscription, so the monthly number is the number. This index tracks the published price of every major route in one place. Sort yourself: cheapest proven protocol at the top, maximum-strength specialist compounds at the bottom.
