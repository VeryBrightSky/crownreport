NOW BEFORE

Tip: matched lighting, angle, and hair length make the honest comparison. Judge at month 3 and month 6, not week 2.

How to shoot photos worth comparing

Same room, same overhead light, same distance, hair dry and cut to a similar length. Shoot the hairline straight on, the crown from above, and one three quarter angle, then repeat the exact setup every 4 weeks. The reason so many people think nothing is happening is that week to week comparison hides changes that month 0 versus month 4 makes obvious. Our regrowth timeline shows what to expect at each checkpoint, and if the drain has you worried in the meantime, the shed checker reads whether it is the normal shed.
