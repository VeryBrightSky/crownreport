How Long Does Minoxidil Take to Work?

5 min read · By The Crown Report Research Desk · we may earn a commission from marked partner links (details)

Quick answer

Expect a shed at weeks 2 to 8, the first fine growth around months 3 to 4, and a fair judgment call at month 6. Full results land near month 12. Quitting during the early shed is the most common mistake people make.

The short version: nothing visible for the first two months, and often a shed instead. Fine new growth around months three to four. Real judgment at month six with matched photos. Full results near twelve months. Anyone promising faster is selling lighting, not hair.

Month by month, what is actually happening

Weeks 1 to 8. Minoxidil pushes resting follicles back into their growth phase, and the old hairs sitting in those follicles get evicted first. That is the shed: more hair in the drain, not less. It looks exactly like failure and it is the single most common reason people quit. Our shed checker tells you in thirty seconds whether what you are seeing fits the normal pattern.

Months 3 to 4. Shedding settles. Fine, short, lighter hairs appear along the hairline and at the crown. They look like baby hair because that is what they are. This is the first honest evidence it is working.

Months 4 to 6. Those fine hairs thicken and darken as they cycle. Density starts changing in a way photos can capture, which is why month six is the standard decision point rather than month two.

Months 6 to 12. Consolidation. Most of the visible gain people show in before-and-afters lands in this window. The full timeline tool walks the whole arc.

Why it cannot be faster

Hair grows roughly a centimeter a month and follicles cycle on their own schedule. No concentration, no shampoo, and no supplement changes that biology. Stronger formulas like 8% minoxidil may produce more growth, not faster growth; we cover that in 8% vs 5%.

How to judge it honestly

Same room, same overhead light, same distance, dry hair, every four weeks. Compare month zero to month four, never week to week, because week-to-week comparison hides real change and feeds panic. The photo comparer puts your two shots in one frame with a slider.

And the part people forget: stopping resets everything. Gains fade over the following months and loss resumes its previous pace, because minoxidil manages the cycle rather than curing the cause. Pairing it with a DHT blocker is what makes results hold, which the budget guide maps at every price.
