Hair Loss Statistics 2026

Updated 1 August 2026 · By The Crown Report Research Desk · we may earn a commission from marked partner links (details)

Quick answer

Around 95% of male hair loss is genetic pattern loss. Finasteride cuts DHT by roughly 70% and dutasteride by roughly 90%. Sexual side effects appeared in 3.8% of men on finasteride versus 2.1% on placebo. Visible results take 3 to 6 months and full results near 12, and the cheapest proven route runs $10 to $25 a month.

How to read these

Percentages from trials describe groups, not you. A 90% response rate means nine in ten participants saw measurable improvement under trial conditions, not that you have a 90% chance of a dramatic result. "Improvement" in most of these studies means shedding stopped and existing hairs thickened; regrowth on fully bare scalp is a different and much smaller outcome. The realistic expectations guide covers that gap in detail, and the price index is where the cost figures come from.
